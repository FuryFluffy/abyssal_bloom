**Scope:** Starting trio + all currently locked Layer 1 and Layer 2 enemies.

**Total:** 14 subjects × 8 states = **112 complete positive/negative prompt pairs**.

## Production rules used throughout

- These prompts are written for **AOM3 / AbyssOrangeMix3 SD1.5**, with **no style LoRA**.
- Load the appropriate character LoRA for Lysandra, Mira, or Seraphine and keep the trigger tag in the prompt: `ablysandra`, `abmira`, `abseraphine`.
- Recommended test canvas: **512×768** or **576×832**. Preserve the same canvas and crop for every state of a subject.
- Generate one sprite per image. The cyan background is intended for easy masking; remove it after curation and export transparent PNGs.
- The attached Layer 1 rooms use a fixed, slightly elevated illustrated perspective with large depth bands. The prompts therefore avoid extreme foreshortening, wide attack arcs, floor-dependent shadows, and poses that change the sprite footprint too much.
- Keep spell projectiles, impact flashes, attack trails, blood, shadows on the floor, selection rings, and status effects as separate Godot effects. The character sprites below contain only the body, equipment, and pose.
- For seed control, first lock a good idle seed for each orientation, then reuse or lightly vary it for attack/hurt/grappled batches. Front and back will usually need separate seed families.

## Suggested output naming

`<code>_<front|back>_<idle|attack|hurt|grappled>_<seed>.png`
